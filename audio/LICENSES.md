# Audio Assets & Open-Source Model Licenses (`audio/LICENSES.md`)

The bundled music and effects are generated by the repository's asset builder rather than downloaded sample packs or commercial recordings. The builder declares a CC0 dedication for its generated assets. Model and software licenses are listed separately below; they are not a blanket license for every generated voice or external service. This inventory does not promise that a platform will never make a Content ID claim.

The multilingual `msedge-tts` fallback calls Microsoft's hosted Edge Read Aloud service. Its wrapper is MIT licensed; that does not make the hosted service or its voices open source or CC0. Service availability and usage terms are separate from the wrapper license.
